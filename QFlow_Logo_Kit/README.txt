QFlow logo kit (version 1, 14 October 2026)

The idea: the Q is the queue.
  - The ring is the letter Q, and the place where you get helped.
  - The yellow tail is you, at the front of the line. Your turn is next.
  - The two small dots are the other people still waiting.

What is in this folder
  svg/    The original vector files. Use these for printing and for anything big. They do not need any font installed.
  png/    Ready-to-use pictures: app icons, browser tab icons, the logo with the name, and one-colour versions.
  sheet/  The three pages of the brand sheet as pictures. QFlow_Logo_Sheet.pdf is the same three pages.

Our colours
  QFlow Blue #1A3FD6  (main colour)
  Night      #0B1F6B  (dark backgrounds and text)
  Next       #FFC83D  (yellow: only for the tail of the Q)
  Mist       #DCE4FF  (light backgrounds)

Which file for what

  iPhone home screen ......... png/apple-touch-icon-180.png
  Browser tab ................ png/favicon-16.png, favicon-32.png, favicon-48.png (small versions leave out the two dots)
  White background ........... png/wordmark-color.png
  Blue background ............ png/wordmark-reversed.png
  Black and white only ....... png/wordmark-black.png, png/wordmark-white.png
